A new contracting
house for the
Kingdom's

Shield is a Saudi construction and contracting company supporting oil & gas, petrochemical, power, infrastructure and government sectors across the Kingdom.

Founded by professionals with deep field experience, our ambition is simple: deliver large-company quality with the speed, focus and accountability of a dedicated partner.

Every engagement is backed by structured quality systems, strict HSE compliance, rapid mobilization and disciplined project execution — from a single trade package to full turnkey scope.
